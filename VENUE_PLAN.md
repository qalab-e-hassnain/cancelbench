# Venue plan

The best topical fit is ACM CUI 2027 because the paper studies cancellation semantics and observable effects in conversational interfaces. The official site lists July 26–28, 2027 in Swansea and says paper dates are still to be announced: https://cui.acm.org/2027/

The best scheduled target is ACL 2027 short paper through the ACL Rolling Review process. Its scope includes dialogue and interactive systems, spoken language understanding, human–AI interaction, and LLM agents. The ARR deadline is January 4, 2027: https://2027.aclweb.org/calls/main/

Use arXiv as a free public preprint after independent review. arXiv moderation and endorsement are not peer review. Follow the selected conference's dual-submission policy before posting or committing the paper.

Submission is generally free for ACL/ARR and CUI, but acceptance normally requires author registration for the paper to appear in proceedings. Travel, registration, and possible publication charges are separate from submission fees. Confirm current fees and waiver policies on the official venue page.

Submit to CUI when its call opens if the paper remains a reference-model/evaluation contribution. Submit to ACL 2027 only after adding a stronger benchmark or real speech/tool experiment; the current oracle-intent simulation is likely too narrow for the main ACL track.

Before submission: complete human review; compile and inspect the PDF; prepare the venue template and anonymous version; tag the public artifact; retain accurate AI-assistance disclosure; and exclude API keys, private data, and speech recordings.
